About UpLinkAI

Built by a student,
for students who refuse

UpLinkAI is a CareerTech & Workforce Development platform helping students move from learning to earning — through real internships, projects, mentorship, and industry exposure.

🏆 NCIIPC × Startup India AI Grand Challenge — Participant 📜 MSME Registration Initiated · Udyam Portal 👥 Real Human Support
Who We Are

A founder-operated platform, built in public

UpLinkAI is a founder-operated CareerTech & Workforce Development platform — not a registered company, but a working product with real students, real partners, and real outcomes. We exist to close the gap between classroom learning and the skills employers actually look for.

🎯

What we actually do

We run cohort-based skilling programs, hackathons, internship placements, and a digital skills curriculum for school and college students — every program backed by a real person on the other end, not an automated workflow.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To give every student — regardless of college tier or city — structured access to internships, mentorship, and real-world projects, so that learning translates directly into earning.

Our Vision

A future where career-readiness is not a privilege of a few campuses, but a standard built into every student's journey — practical, verifiable, and student-first by design.

Our Journey

From an idea to a working platform

A real, ongoing build — every milestone here is one we can stand behind.

The starting problem

Founder experiences firsthand how hard it is for students outside top-tier institutions to access real internships and mentorship — and starts building UpLinkAI to fix that.

First cohorts & hackathons

UpLinkAI runs its first structured cohorts and hackathons/ideathons, bringing students from IIT, NIT, VIT, BITS and other institutions onto the platform.

National recognition

UpLinkAI is accepted as a participant in the NCIIPC × Startup India AI Grand Challenge, co-organised by DPIIT & FITT, IIT Delhi.

Formalising the foundation

MSME registration initiated on the Government of India's Udyam Portal, alongside the build-out of internships, the Digital Skill Hub, and partner collaborations.

Today

UpLinkAI continues to operate as a lean, founder-led team, adding new programs, partners, and verified milestones as the platform grows.
